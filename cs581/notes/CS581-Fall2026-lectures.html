Lectures for CS 581, Fall 2026

The lectures in the first part of course (up to the midterm) cover the basis of the material. The second part of the course will include advanced material (currently deep learning papers in phylogenetics) and will include presentations by students and some guest lectures.

Starting after the midterm, each student will be assigned to present a paper and lead a discussion. All students will be required to read the papers that are presented and participate actively in the discussion of the papers.
